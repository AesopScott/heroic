# Cold Tree

## School Identity

- Base Damage: Standard
- Cast Style: Standard, 1 sec
- Area Shape: Many
- Range: Short
- Cooldown: Slow
- Proc 1: Slow
- Proc 2: Freeze

Cold is the control school. It wins by controlling space, reducing enemy speed, and setting up freeze windows for other skills to exploit.

## 1. Frost Ring
A ring of cold that expands outward from the caster.

- Role: close-range area control
- Base: standard damage, broad zone, short range

### Upgrade Path A: Wider Ring
- Larger radius
- Better for swarms

### Upgrade Path B: Heavier Chill
- Stronger slow
- Better for lock-down play

### Upgrade Path C: Deep Freeze
- Higher chance to freeze
- Best for defensive builds

## 2. Ice Shard
Sharp shards of ice launch into targets and puncture enemy lines.

- Role: single-target pressure with crowd overlap
- Base: standard damage, short range, slow cooldown

### Upgrade Path A: More Shards
- More projectiles
- Better swarm coverage

### Upgrade Path B: Piercing Shards
- Shards pass through enemies
- Stronger line pressure

### Upgrade Path C: Shatter Damage
- Bonus damage against slowed or frozen targets
- Stronger combo payoff

## 3. Glacial Field
A cold zone that covers space and forces enemies to fight slowly.

- Role: battlefield control
- Base: standard damage, many-area coverage, short range

### Upgrade Path A: Wider Field
- Bigger zone
- Better space denial

### Upgrade Path B: Longer Field
- Lasts longer
- Better sustained control

### Upgrade Path C: Deeper Chill
- Stronger slow and attack slowdown
- Best for safety builds

## 4. Crystal Prison
Ice structures lock enemies in place or force them into bad movement.

- Role: hard control and trap setting
- Base: standard damage, slow cooldown, short range

### Upgrade Path A: More Prisons
- More ice traps
- Better coverage

### Upgrade Path B: Faster Trigger
- Traps activate sooner
- Better reaction value

### Upgrade Path C: Hard Lock
- Stronger freeze or root
- Best for elite control

## 5. Shatter Line
A clean line of freezing force that punishes grouped enemies.

- Role: lane clear and freeze burst
- Base: standard damage, long line, short range

### Upgrade Path A: Wider Line
- Easier to catch enemies

### Upgrade Path B: Longer Line
- More reach
- Better for lane shaping

### Upgrade Path C: Brutal Shatter
- Deals bonus damage to chilled or frozen targets
- Best as the finisher of a control combo

## Cold Play Pattern

Cold should feel like:

- slowing enemies first
- freezing them when they overcommit
- making safe zones for the player
- enabling damage from other schools

## Cold Design Rule

Cold should rarely be the highest raw damage school. Its power comes from:

- control
- survivability
- combo setup
- making enemy movement predictable
